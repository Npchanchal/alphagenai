# 100X.VC — supporting pack

**Fixed offer they publish:** ₹1.25 crore, iSAFE, 15% of future equity.  
**Process they publish:** apply, hear back in 7–10 working days, interview in Mumbai, term sheet, diligence, wire about two weeks after documents are in.  
**Apply:** https://www.100x.vc/apply · iSAFE explainer: https://www.100x.vc/isafe

An iSAFE, in their own description, is documented as compulsorily convertible preference shares and converts on a priced round, a liquidity event, or at three years, whichever their note says. **Have a company secretary read the current template.** This pack is not legal advice.

## Decide this before you apply

| Path | Capital | Dilution shape |
|---|---|---|
| 100X | ₹1.25 crore now | 15% of future equity, no valuation today |
| IAN / LetsVenture | ₹75 lakh–₹3 crore | Priced, often less dilution if the cap table is clean |
| Antler Direct | about ₹4 crore | about 11%, if they offer the standard deal |

Taking 100X does not block a later seed. It does consume 15% before that seed exists. If you expect a Surge or Accel round inside a year, price that 15% against the larger round before you fly to Mumbai.

## Mumbai interview, 20 minutes

1. Open Infosys. Two links. One minute of silence while they look.
2. Who pays: a research head, ₹45,000 a seat, after a free pilot.
3. What ₹1.25 crore adds: more closed years per company, fifteen desks, counsel memo.
4. What you will not do: retail, tips, a hired sales team before three active pilots.
5. Ask: “What does the investment committee still need from me this week?”
