## 2 — What it does

We take the quantified guidance from an Indian filing or call, take the actual from the later filing, and publish a 0–100 score with both links. A person accepts the quotes. The model only proposes them.

Look at one row: https://citealpha.com/companies/infy

## 3 — Who it’s for

The analyst who currently keeps that table in Excel. PMS, AIF, sell-side. A quant desk can take the same rows by API.

We charge about ₹45,000 per seat per year, or ₹25–80 lakh for the feed. Those are list prices. We have not charged anyone. A pilot is free for 60 days.

## 5 — How far

Live product. Screener, dossier, search that cites or refuses, API, changelog. AWS. Indian private limited company.

## 6 — Money

This is a seat and a data license. A ten-person desk is about ₹4.5 lakh a year. One serious API is worth more than a year of seats. Year one we want fifteen design partners and one or two licenses. That is the plan, not a pipeline.

We will not sell to retail. The Indian research-analyst rules are not something we are going to discover after a consumer launch.

## 7 — Competition

Marvin Labs and FinCatch do the US version. Indian sites do ratios or sentiment. The asset is the accepted Indian history, not the chat window.
