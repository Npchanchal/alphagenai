## 2 — The model’s job, and the limit

A model can find a guided range in a 90-page results release. It cannot be the author of a published index. CiteAlpha uses the model to propose the quote. A reviewer accepts it. The score is arithmetic over accepted rows. If the guidance quote is missing, the row stays out.

That is the product: extract, bind, refuse.

**Say:** Accel has seen a year of chat-over-PDF companies. The refusal rule is the difference. Show it.

## 4 — Why this data, why India

Indian primary disclosures are a bad fit for a general model: two exchanges, issuer PDFs, slide appendices, mixed language, units that change between the guide and the result. The training advantage is not a bigger model. It is accepted pairs: this sentence was the guide, this sentence was the actual, this label was right.

Every accepted row is an eval item. The corpus gets more valuable as the index gets deeper. That is the compounding loop.

**Say:** Call it an eval set that happens to be a product a desk will pay for. Partners understand evals.
