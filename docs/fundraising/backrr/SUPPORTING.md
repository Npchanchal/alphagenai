# Backrr — supporting pack

Backrr is a contact file of Indian family offices and VCs. The supporting document those offices will forward inside the family is the memo below. The deck is what you leave behind.

Do not scrape, guess, or pattern-build email addresses. Open Backrr, pick offices whose public profile says financial services, data, or India public markets, and write to the address listed there. Eight letters a week.

## Who to select inside Backrr

| Prefer | Skip |
|---|---|
| Single-family offices with a public-markets or financial-services operating business | Offices that only publish real-estate and hospitality deals |
| Cheques of ₹1–5 crore at seed, or “flexible” | “Series B onwards” |
| A principal who will meet | A generic info@ if a named principal is also listed |
| India entity, rupee decision | A fund that needs a Delaware topco before the first meeting. That is the YC path, not this one |

## Do not

- Mail two hundred offices in a day.
- Attach a hockey-stick model.
- Use the words alpha, multibagger, or undervalued.
- Send a sample score on a company the family controls, unless they ask. If they ask, use only public filings and the same method. Do not flatter the family business.
