## 3 — How it makes money

Institutions pay. A research seat is ₹45,000 a year, list price. A data license is ₹25–80 lakh a year, list price. A free pilot is 30–60 days. We are pre-revenue. The round is what gets the first licenses into a conversation with a full Sensex history behind them.

There is no retail app in this plan.

**Say:** One license at the bottom of the range is ₹25 lakh. You need a handful, not a million users. Say the handful.

Public labeling target: Sensex 30 and Nifty 50 at established depth by 31 March 2027. If the bench is smaller, that date moves. We will say so in the quarterly letter.
