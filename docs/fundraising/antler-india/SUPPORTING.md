## Partner meeting

1. Path: Direct or residency. Ten seconds.
2. Demo. Two minutes.
3. The week-four kill test on pilots.
4. Founder paragraph.
5. Their question. You will know it is real when they ask who the first five desks are. Have five names you can actually email. No logos of firms that have not replied.

## Terms to read before you celebrate

- What 11% means: primary, fully diluted, option pool included or not.
- Whether a program fee or a stipend netting exists. YC publishes that it does not charge a fee. Do not assume Antler’s note matches. Read it.
- Follow-on reserve is not follow-on obligation.
- Exclusivity while they decide. Ask how long they need. A four-week exclusive that blocks YC’s 2 November 2026 deadline is a bad trade. YC’s on-time window is the priority if both are live in the same fortnight.
