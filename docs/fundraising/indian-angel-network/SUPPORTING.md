## Application answers

**Describe the startup.**  
CiteAlpha publishes the Guidance Credibility Index for Indian listed companies. The index scores whether management delivered on quantified guidance. Each point cites the filing that guided and the filing that reported the actual. The company is Ocotillo Innovation Private Limited. The product is live at citealpha.com.

**Problem.**  
Institutional analysts track guidance in personal notes. There is no shared, cited record of delivery across years. Indian market products cover fundamentals and sentiment. US guidance trackers do not cover NSE/BSE practice.

**Solution.**  
Extract the quantified promise, match the subsequent actual, label the outcome, and roll accepted rows into a 0–100 score with a confidence tier. A reviewer accepts both quotes before a row can score. The screener, dossier, workbench, filing search, and API are in production.

**Customers.**  
PMS, AIF, sell-side research teams, and quant desks. We are pre-revenue. The first commercial step is 15–20 unpaid pilots of 30–60 days.

**Revenue model.**  
Desk seats at an illustrative ₹45,000 per seat per year. Enterprise API at an illustrative ₹25–80 lakh per year. Pilots are complimentary.

**Market.**  
Beachhead is Indian institutional research budgets, not retail brokerage users. Expansion is the same dataset sold by API to global EM desks.

**Competition.**  
Marvin Labs and FinCatch score guidance delivery on US filings. Tijori and Screener cover Indian filings and ratios. Trendlyne covers sentiment and estimates. None of those is an India guidance-delivery index. We do not replace a market terminal.

**Traction.**  
Live production product. Public changelog for score moves. Pre-revenue. Sensex is the scored universe; Nifty 50 is in progress. Established-depth target: 31 March 2027.

## Questions members will ask

**Why will a desk pay when Screener is cheap?**  
Screener answers “what did they report?” We answer “what did they guide, and did the later filing meet it?” Those are different lines in an initiation note.

**Why won’t Trendlyne add this?**  
They can. The work is the reviewed history, the metric dictionary for Indian line items, and the habit of showing the tier when the history is thin. A feature launch without that history is an empty column.

**What if SEBI says the score is a research report?**  
Then we sell it under whatever registration counsel requires, or we narrow the product to a data file of quoted pairs. We will not discover that after we have taken retail subscriptions. Retail is off now.
