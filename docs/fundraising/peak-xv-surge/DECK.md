## 2 — Insight

Management guidance is a time series. Desks treat it as a sentence. In the US, Marvin Labs and FinCatch already turn the sentence into met, missed, or dropped. In India the filings exist, the calls exist, and the time series does not.

The company that labels that series becomes the reference. The company that only wraps a model does not.

**Say:** This is the only “insight” slide. Do not add a second one.

## 6 — Why now

Models are good enough to propose a quote and not good enough to be the system of record. The window is the labeling, while US vendors are still US-shaped and Indian data vendors are still metric-shaped. Waiting for revenue before labeling means the history never gets deep enough to sell.

**Say:** “Why now” is the model’s limit, not a hype sentence about AI.
