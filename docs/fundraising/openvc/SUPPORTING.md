# OpenVC — supporting pack

OpenVC does not write a cheque. It tells you who might. The work is the filter and twenty good emails, not a blast.

**Upload:** the deck in `DECK.md`, as PDF.  
**Profile one-liner:** Guidance-delivery index for Indian listed companies. Both filings on every point. Pre-revenue. Raising ₹3–5 crore.

## Filters

Set these, then export a list you will actually read.

| Filter | Setting |
|---|---|
| Stage | Pre-seed, seed |
| Cheque | $100k to $2 million |
| Geography | India. Then a second pass: global funds that mark India or emerging markets |
| Sector | Fintech, SaaS, AI, data. Skip consumer, crypto, climate-only, food |
| Keyword in thesis | B2B, vertical software, financial infrastructure, alt data, India |

Throw out any fund whose latest public cheque was Series B or whose site says “we do not lead pre-revenue.”

**Weekly quota:** 8 new emails. Not 80. Personalize the first sentence. Stop a thread after one follow-up.

## Do not

- Buy a second database and mail both lists the same week with the same text.
- Claim OpenVC “featured” you.
- Attach the internal competitive memo, the SEBI counsel gap, or AWS invoices to a cold email. Those are for a second call.
- Invent warm intros. If OpenVC shows a partner and you have no mutual, the email above is the whole relationship.
