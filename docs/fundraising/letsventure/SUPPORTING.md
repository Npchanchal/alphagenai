## Diligence answers angels actually send

**Are you SEBI registered?**  
No. The product is a factual delivery record sold to institutions. A counsel memo on the Research Analyst regulations is part of this round. Retail distribution is off.

**How accurate is the model?**  
We do not quote a precision rate. A score is published only after a reviewer accepts the guidance quote and the actual quote.

**Who else does this?**  
Marvin Labs and FinCatch, on US filings. In India, filing-data companies and screener products do not publish this index.

**What do I receive as an angel?**  
Equity in Ocotillo Innovation Private Limited, pro rata with the rest of the syndicate, and a monthly note: companies deepened, pilots opened, pilots still using it in week four.

**Can I see paying customers?**  
Not yet. The demo is the public dossier. The milestone this cheque buys is the first paid seat or a paid pilot extension.
