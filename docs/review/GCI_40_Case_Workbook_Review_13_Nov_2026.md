# Review of the 40-case workbook: GCI v2.5 diffusion against the tech team's methods

**To:** Product owner / Methodology owner · **From:** Tech team · **Date:** 13 Nov 2026

Reviews: *GCI_v2.5_vs_Tech_Team_40_Case_Evaluation.xlsx* (owner, 12 Nov 2026).
Corrected copy: `assets/gci_algorithm/GCI_40_Case_Evaluation_corrected_13_Nov_2026.xlsx`,
generated by `assets/gci_algorithm/repro/case_workbook.py` from the method lab
(`gci_scoring.lab_row_score`, `lab_run`, `lab_behaviour`).

## Summary

The workbook cannot yet be used to choose a method. Three of its four score columns are not
what the named rule produces:

- **"Tech R2+A1"** leaves out amendment A2. The tech proposal is R2 with A1–A5, and A2 scores a
  met floor or ceiling 100. The column is right for R2 with A1 alone.
- **"Range-P"** scores floors and ceilings as point targets, and scores $M in raw dollars. The
  specification measures amounts as a per cent of the guided midpoint.
- **"Legacy v4"** does not reproduce the published scorer in 74 of 80 rows.
- **"v2.5 Diffusion"** matches the owner's formula except at a zero midpoint, where the formula is
  undefined and the sheet fills in different values for the same numbers on its two sheets.

The cases themselves show the main problem with diffusion as a headline. In 16 rows a result
delivered inside the promise scores 0 or 100, and the sheet tags it MISS or BEAT. A leverage
result three times its cap scores 100.

No published level moves. This is a research comparison.

## What we checked

All 80 rows: 20 cases, each with an upside and a downside result, on a percentage sheet and an
amounts sheet. Each rule was re-run with the method lab defaults, which reproduce the published
v4 level and the 12 Nov scan (`test_gci_method_lab.py`).

| Column in the sheet | Rows that differ from the rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| v2.5 Diffusion | 12 | Zero midpoint (cases 3, 9, 16): undefined |
| Tech R2+A1 | 0 against R2 with A1 only; 11 against the tech proposal | A2 left out |
| Range-P | 5 (%), 38 ($M) | One-sided guides scored as points; $M not converted to per cent of midpoint |
| Legacy v4 | 34 (%), 40 ($M) | Not the published scorer |

## Findings on the columns

### R2: A2 is missing

A2 (accepted in the owner's 11 Nov note) gives full credit for a met floor or ceiling. Without it:

- Guided at least 10%, reported 15%: the sheet shows 50. The proposal scores 100.
- Guided at least 0%, reported 5%: the sheet shows 0. The proposal scores 100.
- Guided at most 10%, reported 5%: the sheet shows 50. The proposal scores 100.
- Net debt / EBITDA guided at most 2.5x, reported −2.5x (net cash): the sheet shows 0. The
  proposal scores 100.

The corrected copy shows both columns.

### Range-P: one-sided guidance and amounts

- A met floor or ceiling scores 100 under Range-P (no distance outside, no width). The sheet
  shows 40.
- For amounts the unit is 1% of the guided midpoint. Guided $6–10M, reported $13M is a 37.5%
  miss and scores 0, not 60.
- Range-P's constants (12.5 points per unit, width charge 0.1) were set for revenue growth. On
  amounts they give 0 to a wide range that contains the result (guided $0–10M, reported $10M).
  Amounts need their own calibration (CAL-R2-01, decision D-D) before Range-P can be judged there.

### v4: not the published scorer

The published v4 scores a result inside the range 100 and floors beats at 60.

| Case | Sheet | v4 |
|---|---|---|
| Guided 0–10%, reported 0% | 0 | 100 |
| Guided 6–10%, reported 3% | 12.5 | 38.3 |
| Guided 6–10%, reported 13% | 75 | 82.4 |
| Guided at least 10%, reported 5% | 30 | 0 |

The three choices already entered under *Select Your Prefer* were made against these values.
The corrected copy clears them.

### Diffusion: zero midpoint

`(x − m) / |m|` has no value at `m = 0`. The sheet fills in values without a stated rule, and
they differ between sheets for identical numbers:

- Guided −10 to +10, reported +5: 100 on the % sheet, 62.5 on the $M sheet.
- Guided 0, reported +5: 100 on the % sheet, 75 on the $M sheet.

This is the zero-midpoint fallback the 11 Nov reply asked the owner to specify.

## What the cases show about diffusion

- **Delivered inside the promise, scored as the largest beat or miss.** In 16 rows the result
  sits inside the guided range or meets the bound. For example:
  - Guided 0–10%, reported 0%: 0, tagged MISS.
  - Guided −2% to 8%, reported −2%: 0, tagged MISS.
  - Guided $10–20M, reported $10M: 33.3, tagged MISS.
- **Ceilings read backwards.** Diffusion has no metric direction.
  - Net debt / EBITDA guided at most 2.5x, reported 7.5x: 100, tagged BEAT.
  - Costs guided at most 10, reported 5: 25, tagged MISS.
  - Guidance Bias (diffusion with direction and a 5 pp growth floor) reads both correctly: 0 and 75.
- **19 of the sheet's BEAT / MISS tags are wrong** for the metric's direction or because the result
  is in line. The corrected copy adds a tag column set by the metric's direction.

## What a single-row table cannot test

The objections in the 11 Nov reply are about levels, not rows. The corrected copy adds two sheets.

**Method properties** (`lab_behaviour`, the 12 Nov scan checks):

| Property | v4 | GCI Range | Range-P | Diffusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Points gained by guiding below own expectation | 0.4 | 0 | 0 | 49.4 |
| Level for a record on guidance every year | 100 | 100 | 100 | 50 |
| Level for a record missing and beating by the same amount in turn | 49.2 | 60 | 75 | 50 |

Under diffusion a company on guidance every year and one that alternates misses and beats have the
same level.

**Roll-up on real rows** (Infosys and Cipla, all closed rows, opening guidance, lab defaults):

| Company | v4 (lab) | GCI Range | Range-P | Diffusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Infosys | 83.2 | 88.0 | 85.7 | 59.2 |
| Cipla | 85.2 | 98.2 | 93.0 | 49.1 |

Every accuracy method ranks Cipla, which lands in or near its range each year, above Infosys,
which missed FY24 by 2.6 points and overshot in other years. Diffusion ranks them the other way.
The lab rows carry no revision or lead-time terms, so these are not the engine's levels.

## Problems with the test design

- **About 14 distinct scenarios, not 20.** Cases 5 and 6, 7 and 8, and 11, 12 and 13 are the same:
  open and closed endpoints differ only when the result lands on the bound, and no row tests that.
- **Cases that are not guidance.** "Target ≠ 10%" (case 17) is not a form of guidance. "Logarithmic"
  (case 19) applies no log transform. Case 20 on the $M sheet puts a leverage ratio in $M and uses a
  negative ratio.
- **Every deviation is ±5 units.** The table cannot show the effect of range width, the size of the
  guided figure, cost metrics, two-sided metrics or results exactly on a bound. *Cases to add* in
  the corrected copy covers each, with Infosys FY26 and Cipla FY25 R&D as real rows.
- **Commentary.** Column L repeats one sentence in every row ("R2+A1 caps at 100 or penalizes beat
  symmetrically (100.0)") whatever the values.
- **Notes sheet.** It groups R2 with Range-P as a "proper scoring rule". R2 has no width charge and
  no pinball loss. Range-P is built on the interval score; R2 is not.

## Open item found during the review

`gci_scoring.lab_tag` reads direction from the metric catalog only. A ceiling on a
higher-is-better metric therefore tags a breach as a beat, while `r2_revision_kind` treats any
ceiling as lower-is-better. No scored row uses a floor or ceiling today, so nothing is affected.
It should be settled with A2 before one-sided guidance is labelled. The corrected workbook uses
lower-is-better research metrics for its ceiling cases. They exist in the script only.

## Decisions we need

1. Use the corrected copy, or a version of it, as the comparison of record. Re-enter preferences
   there.
2. Judge the headline on the *Method properties* and *Roll-up* sheets, not on single rows.
3. The decisions in the 11 Nov reply still stand: an accuracy headline with Guidance Bias beside
   it, or diffusion in shadow under its own ruleset id once roll-up, penalties, direction, growth
   floor and the zero-midpoint fallback are specified.
